CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Store policies

This is a pre-launch policy outline. The store owner must review, complete and publish accurate terms before enabling purchases.

Digital delivery

Once payment has been independently verified, digital ZIP files may be shared to the buyer's specified Google account through restricted Google Drive access. Delivery times must be confirmed before accepting orders.

Refunds and cancellations

Refund eligibility, exceptions, timelines and the support contact must be finalized and displayed before checkout. Applicable consumer protection law still applies.

Privacy

Order enquiries may include the buyer's name, email address, payment reference and Google account email for delivery. The store owner must publish a complete privacy notice before collecting customer data.

Licensing

Purchase does not automatically grant redistribution or resale rights. Exact permitted uses must be specified for each product.

Support

The verified customer support email will be added before launch.
